## Findings

### P1: blocks or misleads

None.

### P2: friction

#### UX-25 Short plans list gear with no step for it
- **Screen:** plan for 72-hour to 14-day households.
- **Evidence:**
  - `.data/ux/2026-11-24-2/personas/gulf-hurricane-plan.txt` (14 days): "Pre-cut 5/8" exterior plywood…" is still
    in the gear list.
  - The first pass's steps "Pre-cut and label window protection" and "Assemble the sanitation kit" are gone (they
    were 60-day steps; `.data/ux/2026-11-24/personas/gulf-hurricane-plan.txt` lines 227 and 230).
  - The /code-review of the fix branch also noted the generator "buy and test-run outdoors" step for short plans
    with a generator recommended.
- **What's wrong:** the owner's UX-7 decision drops the 60- and 90-day stages from short plans, so these steps
  went with them. The products stayed.
- **Why it matters:** gear with no step to use it. For a generator, the test-run step carried the "outdoors, 20+
  ft from the house" reminder (the Power section still has the CO rule).
- **Suggested fix (owner decision):** either move these few steps into "This month" for short plans, or drop
  their products too.

### P3: polish

#### UX-7 (still open, now P3) The full plan is still long
- **Evidence:** `personas.json`: 5,995–8,836 words with every section open and a 15–22 page PDF (first pass:
  5,819–8,517 words, 17–22 pages).
- **What changed:** the plan now opens on "This week" (6–10 steps), with This month and Later collapsed. That
  was the owner's decision and it works: one count, and 574–612 words before you open anything.
- **What's left:** the full text and the PDF are the same size.
- **Suggested fix:** a short PDF (This week plus the key numbers) beside the full one.

#### UX-14 (still open) The Terms page reads above grade 8
- **Evidence:** `copy.json`: terms grade 9.3 with 2 sentences over 25 words, sources 8.5.
- **What changed:** the two long safety sentences (45 and 41 words) are split.
- **Suggested fix:** plain-language pass on Terms and Sources, by the owner (legal wording).

#### UX-22 The "Almost done" summary now sits below the Build button
- **Screen:** q-11-budget.
- **Screenshot:** `.data/ux/2026-11-24-2/screens/q-11-budget-375-light-full.png`
- **What's wrong:** moving the consent boxes above the sticky bar (UX-4) pushed the teaser ("Your 2-week plan…
  includes 54 gal, 98,000 calories…") below the button.
- **Suggested fix:** show a one-line version of the teaser above the consent boxes, or drop it.

#### UX-23 Two footer links are narrower than 44 px
- **Evidence:** `metrics.json`: "Privacy" 43×44 and "Terms" 36×44 on every page. The height now passes; the
  width doesn't.
- **Suggested fix:** `min-width: 44px` (or horizontal padding) on `.tap`.

#### UX-24 Short plans show a "Later" heading that only holds "Skills to practice"
- **Evidence:** `personas/solo-renter-plan.txt` line 216; the PDF and email show "Later" followed directly by
  "Skills to practice".
- **Suggested fix:** for short plans, title it "Later: skills to practice", or put the skills under This month.

#### UX-26 "Later" means two things
- **Evidence:** `solo-renter-plan.txt`: the shopping list phase "Later (upgrades)" (line 121) and the Priority
  list stage "Later" (line 216).
- **Suggested fix:** call the shopping phase "Upgrades".
