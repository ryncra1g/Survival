### Evidence for the scores that changed since PR #18

- **1 Front page:** unchanged scores. It now has a small home illustration (house, warm window, checklist, water jug) and "About 5 minutes of questions, and you leave with 3 things to do first." The illustration is 4.5 KB of inline SVG for both illustrations together.
- **8 Sample plan:** C5 and Cl5. Same first screen as a real plan: the reassurance and 3 cards. "Build your own" is the only extra.
- **22 Plan build failed:** C5, E5 and Cl5. A soft amber-tinted Notice with an icon and body-size text, and a "Try again" button that rebuilds.
- **23 Plan first screen:** C5, E5 and Cl5. One idea at a time: no counts but "0 of 3 done", no prices, no Buy boxes, no stats, no save form.
  - T4: the guidance line and Sources link stay under the title, and prices sit behind "How to do it".
- **24 This week, expanded:** C5. Compact cards, one line each.
  - E4: a long list once opened, but each item is one line.
- **25 Shopping list:** C5. Its own section below the fold. "You have N of M gear items" appears only after a tick.
- **26 Chapters:** C5 and Cl5. One icon per chapter and section, more room between cards (12px) and chapters (32px), and no borders on the cards.
- **27 Section open:** Cl5. Lines are capped at 65 characters.
- **28 Compare options:** C5 and Cl5.
  - Per choice: the name, the price range and one line ("$152 less"; "$260 more, $168 over your budget").
  - The whole-list total appears once, at the top, with "Prices are estimates."
- **30 Save bar:** C5 and Cl5. It's below the fold, and its errors and "sent" state use the Notice.
- **34 Account:** C5. Notices instead of bordered boxes; the delete confirmation uses a soft tint.
- **39 My places:** E5. A failed save offers "Try again".

## Automated checks

- **axe:** 0 violations on 204 captures (375 and 768, light and dark).
- **Layout:** no sideways scroll, no tap targets under 44px, and no screen with more than one amber button.
  - The first capture found a 6px sideways scroll on 28 plan captures at 375px. Closed section cards kept the layout of a two-column table. Fixed in `4c7f0ab`, and `e2e/design.spec.ts` now checks for sideways scroll at 375px.
- **Lighthouse mobile:** 5 key pages, each audited in its own process with a fresh browser and nothing else running. SEO 63 on plan and questionnaire pages is the `noindex` on private pages, by design.

Accessibility is 100 and best practices 100 on all 5, before and after.

**Why the earlier full run failed:**
- The 29-page run timed out on every page ("Network.enable timed out"). An earlier run had been reported as stopped, but only its shell was; its browser kept the script's fixed debugging port.
- The script now picks a free port per run, can audit named pages one at a time, and closes its browser in every case.
- **The speed drops reported in PR #18** (front 95, Q2 92, Q3 90) came from that leftover run and low memory, not from the code.
- **Q7:** a first single-page run scored 82 (CLS 0.33). That run measured an offline-only case, because the audit wrote the draft to the browser but not to the server. The audit now saves the draft as the app does.
