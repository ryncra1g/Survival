### P3

#### UX-23 (still open) Two footer links are narrower than 44 px
- **Evidence:** `metrics.json`: "Privacy" is 43×44 and "Terms" 36×44 on every page. The height passes; the width
  doesn't.
- **Suggested fix:** `min-width: 44px` on `.tap`.

#### UX-27 (new) The taller sticky bar starts with the Terms box underneath it
- **Screen:** q-11-budget, 375×812.
- **Screenshot:** `.data/ux/2026-11-25/screens/q-11-budget-375-light.png`
- **What's wrong:** with the "Almost done" line inside the sticky bar (owner item 5), the bar is about 3 lines
  taller. On first view the Terms box sits under it.
- **Why it's only P3:** tapping Build my plan with the box unticked scrolls to it, focuses it and shows the message
  beside it (UX-4, covered by e2e).
- **Suggested fix:** shorten the line to "54 gal water · 98,000 calories · 18+ sections" (one line), or show it
  only once both boxes are ticked.
