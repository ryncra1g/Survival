# UX pass 2026-11-24

Build `542324c` on `claude/readyplan-emergency-app-kge8mc` (production build, demo mode). Brand set in the shell
only (`NEXT_PUBLIC_BRAND_NAME=ReadyReport`). Flags off, as at launch; the accounts screens come from a separate
test-only server with `ACCOUNTS_ENABLED` and `DIY_TIPS_ENABLED` on. Run with `/ux-pass`
(`.claude/skills/ux-pass/`). Screenshots and raw data are in `.data/ux/2026-11-24/` (local, not committed; rerun
the skill to regenerate them).

## Summary

**P1: 3 · P2: 10 · P3: 8**

- The accessibility and speed basics are clean. axe found no violations on any of 192 screen captures (48 screens ×
  2 sizes × light/dark). Lighthouse mobile gives accessibility 100 and performance 95–100 on all 29 pages. Nothing
  scrolls sideways at 375 or 768.
- The P1s are about honesty, not layout:
  - the places page promises saving that can't happen while accounts are off;
  - the front page promises "exact" amounts that the plan itself calls estimates;
  - the fuel advice contradicts itself.
- The biggest friction is size. Every household gets 51–84 steps, 5,800–8,500 words and a 17–22 page PDF, even a
  single renter. Three different step counts appear on the first screen of the plan.
- Generic family wording ("helmets for the kids", "come get us") reaches one-person households.

## Findings

### P1: blocks or misleads

#### UX-1 Places page promises saving that doesn't exist at launch
- **Screen:** places (`/plan/<id>/places`), 375 light; same at 768.
- **Screenshot:** `.data/ux/2026-11-24/screens/places-375-light-full.png`
- **What's wrong:** under the address form it says "This address isn't saved. Confirm your email to save your
  places and use them on any device." With `ACCOUNTS_ENABLED` off (launch), confirming an email doesn't make saving
  possible: `/account/places` returns 404 (`lib/server/accountPage.ts`).
- **Why it matters:** someone confirms their email expecting to keep their evacuation address. It isn't there, and
  they find out when they need it. This is the "honest copy" rule. (The line was the accepted default for D4 question
  2, written for when accounts ship.)
- **Suggested fix:** while accounts are off, say "This address isn't saved. Print this page and keep a copy in each
  go-bag." Show today's line only when the flag is on.

#### UX-2 Front page promises "exact" amounts; the plan calls them estimates
- **Screen:** front, "How it works" step 2 and the Supplies card; compare plan-top footer and Sources.
- **Screenshots:** `.data/ux/2026-11-24/screens/front-375-light-full.png` and `plan-top-375-light-full.png`
- **What's wrong:** the front page says "Exact amounts, a week-by-week checklist…" and "Exactly how much water and
  food to keep". The plan footer and `/sources` say "Quantities are planning estimates."
- **Why it matters:** it promises an accuracy the app explicitly disclaims, and the two pages contradict each other.
- **Suggested fix:** "Amounts worked out for your household, with the math shown" (the front page already uses
  this line under the buttons).

#### UX-3 Fuel advice contradicts itself, and can exceed a car's tank
- **Screen:** plan, Do now ("Keep every gas/diesel vehicle above half a tank") vs When you must leave ("keep each
  tank above 15 gallons").
- **Evidence:** `.data/ux/2026-11-24/personas/gulf-hurricane-plan.txt` lines 198 and 1142. Same pair in
  `generous-budget-plan.txt` (15 gal), `rural-wildfire-plan.txt` (7.2 gal) and the evacuation card,
  `screens/plan-card-evacuation-open-375-light-full.png` (3 gal).
- **What's wrong:** two different rules for the same tank. For a 250-mile destination the minimum is 15 gallons,
  which is about a full tank on many cars, so "above 15 gallons" may be impossible. The minimum also ignores tank
  size.
- **Why it matters:** contradictory advice in a plan people are told to trust in an emergency. A household can't
  follow both rules.
- **Suggested fix:** one rule: "Keep at least X gallons (about Y of a tank) — enough to reach <destination> with a
  margin. If your tank holds less, carry a filled, approved can or plan a fuel stop." Change it through
  `/change-rules` with hand-computed tests.

### P2: friction

#### UX-4 Required consent boxes sit below the fold, under the sticky button
- **Screens:** q-05-medical and q-11-budget, 375 light.
- **Screenshots:** `screens/q-05-medical-375-light-full.png` and `screens/q-11-budget-375-light-full.png`
- **What's wrong:**
  - The health-consent box (medical) and both consent boxes (last screen) are below the first screenful, partly
    hidden behind the sticky Back/Next bar.
  - In the walkthroughs the boxes sat 819–952 px down a 812 px screen.
  - The health box appears again on the last screen, and consent ticks aren't kept if the person leaves and returns.
- **Why it matters:** people tap Next or Build my plan, get an error, then have to hunt for a box they didn't see
  (error prevention, visibility of status).
- **Suggested fix:**
  - Put the consent boxes above the teaser on the last screen, and add bottom padding equal to the sticky bar's
    height.
  - On error, scroll to the box and focus it.
  - Show the health box once, on the last screen, as one short line.

#### UX-5 The evacuation screen asks nearly the same question twice, with different names
- **Screen:** q-10-evacuation.
- **Screenshot:** `screens/q-10-evacuation-375-light-full.png`
- **What's wrong:**
  - "Preferred approach" offers "Family or friends / Another property / Hotel or public shelter". The next question
    offers "Friends/family / Second property / Hotel or motel / Public shelter / Raw land".
  - People answer the same choice twice, under different labels.
  - It's the longest questionnaire screen to answer: the destination and distance sat 934–996 px down in the
    walkthroughs.
- **Why it matters:** repeated questions feel like a mistake. Two names for one thing breaks consistency.
- **Suggested fix:**
  - Ask "Stay home when it's safe / Leave" first, then the destination only for people who'd leave.
  - Use one set of labels.
  - Add a hint for "Raw land", e.g. "land you own with no house".

#### UX-6 Three different step counts on the first plan screen
- **Screen:** plan-top.
- **Screenshot:** `screens/plan-top-375-light.png`
- **What's wrong:** "This week 0 of 10 done", "Whole plan: 0 of 88 steps" and "See every step (25 left)" all appear
  above the fold.
- **Why it matters:** people can't tell how much they're being asked to do, or which number to trust (visibility of
  status, minimalist design).
- **Suggested fix:**
  - Show one count: "This week: 0 of 10".
  - Move the whole-plan count into the Do now chapter.
  - Make the link say what it opens: "See all of this week's steps".

#### UX-7 Every plan is very long, whatever the household
- **Screens:** the plan for all 8 households. See the persona table.
- **Evidence:** `.data/ux/2026-11-24/personas/*-plan.txt` and `personas.json`.
- **What's wrong:**
  - 51–84 steps, 5,800–8,500 words and a 17–22 page PDF.
  - The tight-budget household on a 72-hour plan still gets 60 steps, about 6,000 words and 17 pages.
  - The same generator/CO safety paragraph appears twice in every one of the 8 plans.
- **Why it matters:** length works against the "Know what to do" promise. Most people will read only Next up.
- **Suggested fix:**
  - Scale the plan to the scenario length and the household: a 72-hour plan without a generator doesn't need power
    math.
  - Print the safety block once and link to it.
  - Have the PDF lead with a two-page summary.

#### UX-8 Family wording reaches one-person households
- **Screen:** plan (People & plans, hazards, heat) for solo-renter.
- **Evidence:** `personas/solo-renter-plan.txt`:
  - line 314: "Keep bike helmets for the kids in the shelter spot.";
  - line 876: "keep kids and older adults warm first";
  - line 1014: 'agree on the trigger that means "come get us"';
  - line 873: "Everyone sleeps there".
- **What's wrong:** text written for families shows up for a household of 1 adult with no children ("People: 1
  adult, no children" in the same plan).
- **Why it matters:** this breaks the "never assumes a family" rule in CLAUDE.md, and it reads like a template.
- **Suggested fix:**
  - Gate the kid lines on `childrenAges.length`.
  - Use "you" or "your household" in the shared lines.
  - Add a solo-household check to `tests/solo.test.ts` for these phrases.

#### UX-9 Plan controls below 44 px
- **Screens:** plan-top, plan-compare and plan-card-evacuation-open, 375 light; on-plan-tabs (flags on).
- **Evidence:** `metrics.json` and `summary.md` (Layout).
- **What's wrong:**
  - level chips ("✓ Standard") and chapter tabs: 34 px tall;
  - Buy buttons inside steps: 37 px;
  - gear Buy buttons, "Compare options" and "Switch": 40 px;
  - Expand all / Collapse all: 32 px;
  - official-source links under steps: 20 px;
  - pet "Remove": 20 px;
  - flags-on Overview / Inventory / Report tabs: 36 px.

  The questionnaire controls are all fine (52 px choices, 48 px buttons).
- **Why it matters:** these are the most-used controls on the plan, used one-handed on a phone.
- **Suggested fix:** `min-h-[44px]` on chips, tabs, the step Buy box, Compare/Switch, Expand/Collapse and pet
  Remove. Give the source links 44 px rows, or space them as a list.

#### UX-10 The same thing has different names
- **Screens:** plan-save-bar, plan-share-menu, help, plan text.
- **Screenshots:** `screens/plan-save-bar-375-light.png` and `screens/plan-share-menu-375-light.png`
- **What's wrong:**
  - Saving by email is "Confirm email" (save bar), "Email me my plan" (form heading and Share menu) and "Email my
    plan" (button).
  - The go-bag is "Bug-out kit" in the gear list (`rural-wildfire-plan.txt` line 1370) but "go-bag" everywhere else.
  - Gear levels are "basic or premium" on the front page but "Essential / Standard / Premium" in the plan.
  - Help says "Privacy & data" where the footer says "Export or delete my data", and Help says tick "I have this"
    where the list says "Tick what you already have".
- **Why it matters:** people wonder whether these are different features (consistency and standards).
- **Suggested fix:**
  - One name per thing: "Email me my plan" everywhere, "go-bag", "Essential / Standard / Premium".
  - Match the Help wording to the labels people see.
  - Add these pairs to the copy test.

#### UX-11 "New total" in Compare options doesn't match any total shown elsewhere
- **Screen:** plan-compare.
- **Screenshot:** `screens/plan-compare-375-light.png`
- **What's wrong:** Compare says "−$152 · new total $897". Everywhere else the plan total is a range ("$609–$1,488" in
  the Shopping list and Budget by phase), so $897 matches nothing on screen.
- **Why it matters:** people can't check the number, which undermines "the math shown".
- **Suggested fix:** show the new total as a range in the same form as the Shopping list, or say which figure it is
  ("new midpoint total").

#### UX-12 A household with mobility needs is told to buy four 7-gallon jugs
- **Screen:** plan (Supplies) for elderly-mobility.
- **Evidence:** `personas/elderly-mobility-plan.txt` lines 206 and 354: "Finish stored water: 28 gallons (4 7-gal
  jugs)".
- **What's wrong:** a full 7-gallon jug weighs about 58 lb. The household said someone has limited mobility, but the
  container choice doesn't change.
- **Why it matters:** the plan's main water item is one they may not be able to lift or pour.
- **Suggested fix:** when `elderlyOrMobility` is set, default to smaller containers (cases of bottled water, or 2–3
  gallon jugs), and say why.

#### UX-13 A high wildfire-risk household's first steps don't mention fire
- **Screen:** plan-top for rural-wildfire (Paradise, CA ZIP; wildfire rated high).
- **Evidence:** `personas.json`, rural-wildfire `firstSteps`; `rural-wildfire-plan.txt` lines 216 and 432.
- **What's wrong:** the first 3 steps are the well pump, CO alarms and stored water. Alerts are step 4. "Know your
  evacuation zone" is only in the hazards section.
- **Why it matters:** for this household the fastest-moving danger is fire and evacuation, and most people act only
  on Next up.
- **Suggested fix:** when wildfire (or hurricane surge) is high, put "Sign up for county alerts and know your
  evacuation zone" in the top 3. The Gulf plan already does this for hurricanes.

### P3: polish

#### UX-14 Long or hard sentences in a few places
- **Screens:** terms (grade 9.3), sources (8.5), q-05-medical (8.4), maintenance (8.1). Plan text sentences:
  - 41 words, the water disinfection line (`screens/plan-card-water-open-375-light-full.png`);
  - 45 words, the indoor propane heater line (`elderly-mobility-plan.txt`).

  There are 10–14 sentences over 25 words per plan (`copy.json`). Everything else is grade 8 or lower.
- **Suggested fix:** split the long safety sentences into short bullets. They are the ones that matter most.

#### UX-15 Choices without enough help text
- **Screens:** q-06-home, q-08-power and q-01-location.
- **Screenshots:** `screens/q-06-home-375-light.png`, `screens/q-08-power-375-light.png` and
  `screens/q-01-location-375-light.png`
- **What's wrong:**
  - "Floors (not counting a basement)" doesn't say what to count in an apartment: the unit or the building. The
    answer sets the CO alarm count.
  - "Solar / battery" doesn't say whether a portable power station counts.
  - "FEMA recommended" on 2 weeks should be checked against the source and listed in `docs/FACTCHECK.md`.
- **Suggested fix:**
  - Hints: "Floors in your home (for an apartment, floors in your unit)" and "Includes a portable power station".
  - Add a FACTCHECK row for "FEMA recommended".

#### UX-16 A visible box around every questionnaire heading
- **Screens:** all `q-*`.
- **Screenshot:** `screens/q-01-location-375-light.png`
- **What's wrong:** focus moves to the heading on each step (good for screen readers), but the focus ring draws a
  heavy box around the title for everyone.
- **Suggested fix:** keep the focus move, but show the ring only for keyboard focus (`:focus-visible`), or hide it on
  the programmatically focused heading.

#### UX-17 "Your area: Ohio, OH"
- **Screen:** q-01-location.
- **Screenshot:** `screens/q-01-location-375-light.png`
- **Suggested fix:** show the region once ("Ohio") or the metro and state ("Columbus area, OH").

#### UX-18 "TOP PRIORITY:" in capitals
- **Screen:** plan-top (powered-device and rural-wildfire).
- **Screenshot:** `.data/ux/2026-11-24/personas/powered-device-plan-top.png`
- **Suggested fix:** use a small "Most important" label, styled like the "later" tag. All-caps reads as shouting and
  works against the calm tone.

#### UX-19 Device names pasted into a sentence
- **Evidence:** `personas/powered-device-plan.txt`: "Your Oxygen concentrator, CPAP/BiPAP needs battery backup…"
- **Suggested fix:** "Your oxygen concentrator and CPAP/BiPAP need battery backup…" (join with "and", lower-case,
  plural verb).

#### UX-20 Question count differs between pages
- **Screens:** front ("Answer about 10 quick questions"), start ("About 11 quick screens") and the questionnaire
  ("Step 1 of 11"; 10 screens for households without a toddler).
- **Suggested fix:** "About 10 quick screens" everywhere.

#### UX-21 Small footer links
- **Screens:** every page.
- **Evidence:** `metrics.json`: Privacy, Terms, Sources & safety, Export or delete my data and Ready.gov are 20 px
  tall.
- **What's wrong:** they're spaced apart, so they meet WCAG 2.2's 24 px minimum with spacing, but not this pass's
  44 px goal.
- **Suggested fix:** 44 px rows on phones.

## Questions only real users can answer

1. Do people read past "Next up", or is the rest of the plan reference material they never open? (Decides UX-7.)
2. Does "Saved on this device for 30 days" make people confirm their email, or does it read as "already saved, I'm
   done"?
3. Do people recognize "Stay home when it's safe" and "where could you go" as two different questions?
4. Which number do people use to judge cost: the budget band, the Shopping list range, or a Compare total?
5. For older or disabled people, which water containers can they actually lift and pour?
6. Do renters act on "Test or install CO alarms", or stop because they think it's the landlord's job?
7. Do people tick "I have this" accurately, or tick things they mean to buy?
8. Is "Copy for your AI assistant" understood and used, and does the summary help?
9. In a real alert (a storm watch, a fire warning), do people find the Day 0 checklist in under a minute?

## Method and limits

- **Automated:** Chromium only, at 375×812 and 768×1024, light and dark. Real iOS Safari and Android Chrome weren't
  tested.
- **Personas:** simulated by scripts. They show what's tappable and what the plan says, not how a person feels or
  where they hesitate.
- **Lighthouse:** it audits page loads, so menus, expanded cards and the save bar (which share a URL with their page)
  were checked by axe only. The questionnaire steps were audited by preloading each step's saved draft.
- **Screen readers:** no pass with VoiceOver or TalkBack. axe checks structure, not the experience.
- **Plan text:** read for contradictions with targeted searches (amounts, fuel, family terms, home features), not
  line by line for all 8 plans.
- **Scope:** findings are suggestions for the owner. Nothing in the app was changed by this pass.
